# 🎓 AI College FAQ Assistant

An AI-powered College FAQ Assistant designed to help students quickly get answers to common college-related questions.

The system uses a college knowledge base and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to provide relevant answers about attendance, examinations, placements, fees, library facilities, student clubs, hostels and other college-related information.

## 🏫 College

**Bannari Amman Institute of Technology (BIT)**  
Alathukombai, Sathyamangalam,  
Erode District, Tamil Nadu, India.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Students frequently need information about college rules, academic activities, examinations, placements, fees, library facilities and hostels.

Searching through different notices, documents and websites can take time.

The **AI College FAQ Assistant** provides a simple conversational interface where students can ask questions in natural language.

The system searches the college knowledge base and provides a relevant answer.

## 💡 Example Questions

The assistant can answer questions such as:

- What is the minimum attendance requirement?
- When are the semester examinations?
- How can I contact the placement department?
- What are the available student clubs?
- What are the college working hours?
- What is the tuition fee per semester?
- How do I access the library?
- What hostel facilities are available?
